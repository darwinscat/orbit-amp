Amps: all

### Changed
- **The input is unity again, in every amp.** OrbitAmp's IN fader and the INPUT lever of the cockpit and
  the rovers are 0 dB at their middle, and there is no machine setting under them any more: the lever's
  zero is gone, from every menu. How hard a capture is fed is its pack's own to say, and every pack says it
  now — -9 dB on every pack of today's set, where its captures were taken.
- A session or a preset from 0.8.0 plays as it did, and its gate opens on the same guitar. A zero you had
  picked in 0.8.0 other than -9 dB is gone, and those sessions shift by the difference: one made under
  -12 dB plays 3 dB hotter.
- A pack of your own that states no level is fed at unity.
- OrbitAmp feeds the packs of the set 9 dB softer than it did, where they ask to be fed; its sessions keep
  their IN fader and their gate.
