Amps: OrbitBass

### Added
- **OrbitBass, the bass's workstation.** OrbitAmp's frame, blocks and engine over a bass's chain: the gate, the
  captured amp with its own tone, the cabinet and the limiter, one signal from the input to the output, and no
  delay or reverb. Out of the box the gate is off and the cabinet's low cut is on at 25 Hz, under a 5-string's
  low B. Its tuner reads down to a drop-A's A0. Its presets and settings are its own.
