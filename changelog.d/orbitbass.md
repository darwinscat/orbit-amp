Amps: OrbitBass

### Added
- **OrbitBass, the bass's workstation.** OrbitAmp's frame, blocks and engine over a bass's chain: the gate, COMP,
  the captured amp with its own tone, the cabinet with the DI beside it, its own low and high cuts, and the limiter —
  one signal from the input to the output, and no delay or reverb. COMP is one knob: at 0 no compressor at all, up
  the knob harder and lower, a share of the untouched bass kept under it; its menu sets the attack, the release and
  the character. BLEND, beside the amp's GAIN, keeps the low end clean under a crossover (80–250 Hz) and drives only
  the top. DI/CAB mixes the bass before the cabinet with the cabinet's. The HPF goes by the strings — 5 or 4 — and the
  LPF by the hand — FINGERS or SLAP — each frequency set in its menu. Its tuner reads down to a drop-A's A0. Its
  presets and settings are its own.
