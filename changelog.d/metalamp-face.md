Amps: MetalAmp

### Added
- **MetalAmp, a new guitar amp (a working name): the ENGL E530 preamp's face in the metal world's cabin.** A lever
  of five stops drawn as glyphs, PARK (the amp's bypass) over four channels, CLEAN GAIN LO and HI, LEAD GAIN LO and
  HI, the full name under the hand and on the tuner's screen as it lands; GAIN; the playing pack's tone knobs and
  keys, as many as it has, drawn by code; COMP and its squeeze; FX 1 (the delay), FX 2 (nothing yet) and REVERB; IN
  and OUT; GATE and LIMITER; the cabinet's slider and the amp at work under it; the history and the four registers.
  Its words in DC Rocker, a European subset of New Rocker (SIL OFL 1.1) renamed as its licence asks. The cat in the
  maker's plate is brushed silver, and crimson in flames under the hand. Asked for by name; not in the family's
  release.
- **A stand-in sound until the E530's own captures come:** the Victory V4's released packs, CLEAN GAIN LO the
  Copper, CLEAN GAIN HI the Sheriff, LEAD GAIN LO the Kraken, LEAD GAIN HI the Kraken at a hotter Gain. The window
  shows the Mars plains until the metal world comes.
