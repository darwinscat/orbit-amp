Amps: CatpitAmp, RedPawAmp, FrostPawAmp

### Changed
- **One badge.** RedPawAmp and FrostPawAmp wear CatpitAmp's badge now, scaled to their own emblem spot: a
  wide flat top, sides that slant down to a narrower bottom with a shallow point in the middle, a fine
  bevel, six screws and the cat engraved light into it. On Mars the plate is close to the metal round it and
  the cat ginger, like the plains outside; on the ice moon the plate is a shade darker than the steel round
  it. Every badge, the cockpit's too, is lightly scratched, like metal in use.
