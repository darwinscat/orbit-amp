Amps: RedGrooveBass

### Added
- **RedGrooveBass, the walker.** A bass amp in the cabin of a walker striding through a glowing mushroom forest, seen
  through its glass dome. Two gears, B15 and SVT, each an amp of its own: the lever, the 1 and 2 keys, a Program
  Change or the host's CHANNEL picks one, and each keeps its own DRIVE, BLEND, tone, COMP, effects, DI/CAB and
  cabinet. COMP is one knob, its threshold — clockwise the harder — at 4:1 on OPTO, its squeeze shown beside it. BLEND
  keeps the low end clean under the drive. BASS, MIDDLE and TREBLE, with MIDDLE's frequency on a slider from 200 Hz to
  3 kHz. OCTAVE, ENVELOPE and CHORUS each on a slider of its own, all the way down off. The cabinet on a screen — a
  click lists the cabinets, a right click opens their window — DI/CAB beside it, and two switches: the strings, 5 STR
  or 4 STR, and the hand, FINGERS or SLAP, each with its frequency on a right click. GATE, LIMIT, the tuner down to a
  drop-A's A0, UNDO, REDO and the A B C D registers, as every amp of the family has them; its presets and settings
  are its own.
- **The forest answers the bass.** The walker strides in its gear and in time with the host's tempo, and the forest
  lights with every note — on from the start on this amp (LANDSCAPE, Lit by the bass, in the settings menu).
