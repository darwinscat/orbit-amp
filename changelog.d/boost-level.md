Amps: all

### Added
- **The boost is louder too** — while an amp's boost is on, the amp leaves +3 dB louder, so a solo stands
  out of the band. How much is picked with a right click on the boost itself — -3, 0, +3, +6 or +9 dB:
  OrbitAmp's BOOST key in the AMP section, TURBO on the cockpit (under how hard it pushes) and on the
  rovers. The lift comes at the end of the chain, after the cabinet, the reverb and the delay, ahead of the
  OUTPUT and the limiter, which still holds its ceiling; throwing the boost or picking another level glides
  there in 15 ms instead of stepping. One level for the whole amp, kept in sessions and presets. A device
  with no Boost switch of its own — the X88-IR in OrbitAmp — has no boost to make louder.

### Changed
- **A right click on the rovers' TURBO opens its menu** instead of throwing TURBO.
