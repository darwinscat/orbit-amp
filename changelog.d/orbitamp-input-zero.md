Amps: OrbitAmp

### Changed
- **The IN fader's 0 is the input's zero now, -9 dB unless you say otherwise** — the same setting, by the
  same name, as every amp of the family has. A guitar straight into an interface is hotter than what the
  captures were fed, so out of the box OrbitAmp now plays its input 9 dB down with the fader at 0. Pick
  another in SETUP (the gear), on its new INPUT page, under INPUT'S ZERO: from +12 to -24 dB in steps of 3.
  The fader stays where it is and the sound goes with the zero. It is this machine's setting, not the
  sound's: a preset does not carry it.
- A session or a preset made before, or against another zero, opens with the IN fader where it was and
  plays it over the zero in force — so one from an earlier release plays its input 9 dB quieter than it did,
  unless the zero is set to 0 dB.
