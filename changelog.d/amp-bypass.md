### Added
- **Bypass, on every face.** The whole amp out and the guitar through as it came in, crossfaded, held as late as the
  amp plays so nothing jumps, and the tuner still listening. In the cockpits the POWERED BY plate is the switch and
  goes dark while the amp is out; OrbitAmp and OrbitBass have a power sign at the left of the footer. It is the
  host's bypass too, and no preset, A/B/C/D register or undo step carries it: a preset never switches the amp off.
