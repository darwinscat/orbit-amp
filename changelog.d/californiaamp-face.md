Amps: PeacePhaseAmp

### Added
- PeacePhaseAmp, a new guitar amp: the Orange Super Crush 100's face in a 1967 hippie van, with no
  words on its panels, only pictograms, each named on hover. A lever with PARK (the amp's bypass), CLEAN and DIRTY;
  GAIN, BASS, MIDDLE and TREBLE, each tone knob the channel's tone of that name; COMP and its squeeze; DELAY and
  REVERB; IN and OUT, GATE and LIMITER; the tuner, the cabinet's slider and its picture; UNDO, REDO and A to D.
- The sound is a stand-in until the Orange has captures of its own: CLEAN plays the Victory V4 Copper, DIRTY the
  V4 Sheriff. VOLUME and PHASER are on the panel, dark: the chain has neither yet. Its world is the Mars plains for
  now.
