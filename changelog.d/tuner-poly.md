### Added
- **A polyphonic tuner, as an option.** Strum the open strings and the tuner reads every one of them at once: a lane
  for each string, lowest on the left, with the note it is tuned to and a needle of its own — green when the string is
  home, warming to orange as it goes off. A string you did not play shows a dash; one more than a semitone off says
  FAR; one the tuner cannot tell from another string's harmonic — muted, or in exact unison with it — shows a
  plectrum: pluck that one alone. The reading comes about a second after the strum and sharpens as the strings ring; a
  single plucked string shows in its own lane. It reads through mains hum by itself, so there is no HUM label in this
  mode. Choose POLY in SETUP › TUNER › DISPLAY, or in the tuner's right-click menu under Display, and the tuning next
  to it — standard, drop D, half or whole step down. It runs only while it is chosen and the tuner is open; the needle
  stays the default, and the needle and the strobe are exactly as they were.
