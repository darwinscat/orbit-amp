### Changed
- **The tuner names mains hum for what it is.** A cable humming with nobody playing used to read as a note — 50 Hz as
  G1 +35, 60 Hz as B1 -49, for as long as it hummed. Now, when the reading sits on a mains line (50 or 60 Hz, or their
  2nd to 4th harmonics) and nothing has been plucked, the tuner writes **HUM 50** or **HUM 60** where the note's name
  stands, and dims the needle. Nothing is hidden: the needle still shows what it hears. A string plucked on a line
  reads its note; hum that comes in with a thump reads as a note for two seconds, then as HUM.
