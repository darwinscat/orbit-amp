AmberPawAmp MIDI commands

It listens to every MIDI input that is switched on, on all 16 channels.

Program Change
1	CLEAN
2	CLEAN TURBO
3	CRUNCH
4	CRUNCH TURBO
5	HI-GAIN
6	HI-GAIN TURBO
7	PARK

PARK takes the whole amp out of the sound. A controller that numbers its programs from 0 sends 0 to 6 for these.

Control Change
20	TURBO
21	HPF
22	LPF

64 and up is on, below 64 is off.

23	DRIVE
24	BASS
25	MIDDLE
26	TREBLE

0 to 127 is the knob's whole travel. On a tone knob 64 is the middle.
