# tubebase: a mesh for the world to draw in 3D. The lava-tube base: a skylight pit at the origin, its walls down to -15 m. pit radius 13.0: lower the ground inside r = 13.0 m round the origin (the ragged wall lies between 11.0 and 12.8 m from it; the heaped rim reaches 22 m out, 1-3 m high at its lip). A steel hoist gantry over the pit with its platform hanging at -6.4 m, a railed ramp switchbacking down the south-west wall, three lamps lit deep inside (kind 3), a beacon on the winch house; dormant. credit: none: our own model (Darwin's Cat), no one else's assets
