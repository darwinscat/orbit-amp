# rover: a mesh for the world to draw in 3D. The abandoned Io rover: a 9.6 m shielded hull on six 1.7 m NiTi spring-tyre wheels, the amber hot-cell window in its cab (the eye 2.6 m up), water-jacket tanks, a 5.6 m boom to the fission reactor behind its shadow shield and a fan of radiators; its floods dead (glass), dusted by years of fallout; kinds 0 painted, 1 metal, 2 glass; the footprint centre at the origin, the wheels on z = 0, the front toward -y. credit: none: our own model (Darwin's Cat), no one else's assets
