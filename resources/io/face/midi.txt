IoBass MIDI commands

It listens to every MIDI input that is switched on, on all 16 channels.

Program Change
1	SLOW
2	FAST

A controller that numbers its programs from 0 sends 0 to 1 for these. Both play the same sound: they are the rover's two speeds.

Control Change
23	DRIVE
24	BASS
25	MIDDLE
26	TREBLE

0 to 127 is the knob's whole travel. On a tone knob 64 is the middle.
