AmberPawAmp MIDI commands

It listens to every MIDI input that is switched on, on all 16 channels.

Program Change
1	COPPER
2	COPPER TB
3	SHERIFF 1
4	SHERIFF 2
5	KRAKEN 1
6	KRAKEN 2
7	PARK

The gear is the pedal: CLEAN the Copper, CRUNCH the Sheriff, HI-GAIN the Kraken. TURBO is its second channel. PARK takes the whole amp out of the sound. A controller that numbers its programs from 0 sends 0 to 6 for these.

Control Change
20	TURBO
21	HPF
22	LPF

64 and up is on, below 64 is off.

23	DRIVE
24	BASS
25	MIDDLE
26	TREBLE

0 to 127 is the knob's whole travel. On a tone knob 64 is the middle.
