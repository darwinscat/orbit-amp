MetalAmp MIDI commands

It listens to every MIDI input that is switched on, on all 16 channels.

Program Change
1	CLEAN GAIN LO
2	CLEAN GAIN HI
3	LEAD GAIN LO
4	LEAD GAIN HI

A controller that numbers its programs from 0 sends 0 to 3 for these.

Control Change
23	GAIN
24	the first tone knob
25	the second tone knob
26	the third tone knob

0 to 127 is the knob's whole travel. On a tone knob 64 is the middle.
